About Cedi Tracker

Cedi Tracker is a simple personal finance app for tracking money received and spent, monthly budgets, savings, investments and debts. Version 3.7.

What it does

Daily, weekly and monthly totals, spending by category, budget alerts, linked savings, investment and debt records, Excel and text statements, up to 3 profiles, a PIN lock and daily summaries.

Your data

Everything is stored only on this phone, in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Download a backup regularly from Settings.

Exchange rates

Whenever you are online, the app downloads a public exchange-rate table by itself and keeps it on your phone, so changing currency also works offline. No records or personal information are sent. Rates By Exchange Rate API
